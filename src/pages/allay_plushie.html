About

The allay plushie is a decorative block inspired by the allay mob!

Config

Set Enable Allay Plushie to Yes/true (default) to activate this block, or No/false to disable it.

Placement

When placing an allay plushie, it will face the player.

Breaking

Allay Plushies can be broken with any item or by hand!

Crafting Recipe

Craft the allay plushie by placing 3 light blue wool in the top and bottom rows and an amethyst shard between 2 white wool in the middle row.
