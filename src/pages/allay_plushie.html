About

The allay plushie is a decorative plushie inspired by the allay mob!

Config

Set Enable Allay Plushie to Yes/true (default) to activate this block, or No/false to disable it.

Crafting Recipe

Craft the Allay plushie by filling the top and bottom rows with light blue wool and placing an amethyst shard between two white wool in the middle row.
