About

The cat plushie is a decorative block inspired by the various cat mob variants!

Config

Set Enable Cat Plushies to Yes/true (default) to use them, or No/false to hide them. All cat plushies use this option.

Placement

When placing any cat plushie, it will face the player.

Breaking

Cat Plushies can be broken with any item or by hand!

Interaction

If a player interacts with a cat plushie (use item/place block key), it will alternate between sitting and standing.

Crafting Recipe

Ocelot Plushie

Craft the ocelot plushie by placing 2 yellow wool followed by 1 brown wool in the top row; cod or salmon between 2 green wool in the middle row; and 1 brown wool followed by 2 yellow wool in the bottom row.

White Cat Plushie

Craft the white cat plushie by placing 3 white wool in the top and bottom rows; and yellow wool, a cod or salmon, and light blue wool in the middle row.

Tabby Cat Plushie

Craft the tabby cat plushie by placing a cod or salmon between 2 orange wool in the middle row and 3 brown wool in the top and bottom rows.

Tuxedo Cat Plushie

Craft the tuxedo cat plushie by placing cod or salmon between 2 green wool in the middle row and 3 black wool in the top and bottom rows.

Red Cat Plushie

Craft the red cat plushie by placing cod or salmon between 2 green wool in the middle row and 3 orange wool in the top and bottom rows.

Siamese Cat Plushie

Craft the Siamese cat plushie by placing 3 brown wool in the top row; cod or salmon between 2 black wool in the middle row; and 3 white wool in the bottom row.

British Shorthair Cat Plushie

Craft the British Shorthair cat plushie by placing a cod or salmon between 2 yellow wool in the middle row and 3 light gray wool in the top and bottom rows.

Calico Cat Plushie

Craft the calico cat plushie by placing black wool, white wool, and orange wool in the top row; yellow wool, a cod or salmon, followed by light blue wool in the middle row; and 3 white wool in the bottom row.

Persian Cat Plushie

Craft the Persian cat plushie by placing a cod or salmon between 2 black wool in the middle row and 3 yellow wool in the top and bottom rows.

Ragdoll Cat Plushie

Craft the ragdoll cat plushie by placing white wool between 2 light gray wool in the top row; cod or salmon between 2 light blue wool in the middle row; and 3 white wool in the bottom row.

Black Cat Plushie

Craft the black cat plushie by placing cod or salmon between 2 orange wool in the middle row and three black wool in the top and bottom rows.

Jellie Cat Plushie

Craft the Jellie cat plushie by placing 3 gray wool in the top row; black wool, cod or salmon between two black wool in the middle row; and 3 white wool in the bottom row.
