About

Cat plushies are decorative plushies inspired by the various cat mob variants!

Config

  • In-Game Screen Name: Enable Cat Plushies
  • Config File Key: enable_cat_plushies
  • Default: true / Yes

Determines whether cat plushies can be obtained.

  • true / Yes: Cat plushies are enabled.
  • false / No: Cat plushies are disabled and hidden.

Interaction

A cat plushie can alternate between sitting and standing by using the "Use Item/Place Block" control (default: right mouse button). To place a block against the plushie instead, you must sneak.

Crafting Recipe

Ocelot Plushie

Craft the Ocelot plushie by framing any raw fish in the center with two green wool, two brown wool on opposite corners, and yellow wool filling the remaining slots.

White Cat Plushie

Craft the white cat plushie by filling the top and bottom rows with white wool, and placing yellow wool, any raw fish, and light blue wool from left to right in the middle row.

Tabby Cat Plushie

Craft the tabby cat plushie by filling the top and bottom rows with brown wool, and placing any raw fish between two orange wool in the middle row.

Tuxedo Cat Plushie

Craft the tuxedo cat plushie by filling the top and bottom rows with black wool, and placing any raw fish between two green wool in the middle row.

Red Cat Plushie

Craft the red cat plushie by placing cod or salmon between 2 green wool in the middle row and 3 orange wool in the top and bottom rows.

Siamese Cat Plushie

Craft the Siamese cat plushie by filling the top row with brown wool, the bottom row with white wool, and placing any raw fish between two black wool in the middle row.

British Shorthair Cat Plushie

Craft the British Shorthair cat plushie by filling the top and bottom rows with light gray wool, and placing any raw fish between two yellow wool in the middle row.

Calico Cat Plushie

Craft the calico cat plushie by placing black, white, and orange wool in the top row; any raw fish between yellow and light blue wool in the middle row; and filling the bottom row with white wool.

Persian Cat Plushie

Craft the Persian cat plushie by filling the top and bottom rows with yellow wool, and placing any raw fish between two black wool in the middle row.

Ragdoll Cat Plushie

Craft the ragdoll cat plushie by placing white wool between two light gray wool in the top row; any raw fish between two light blue wool in the middle row; and filling the bottom row with white wool.

Black Cat Plushie

Craft the black cat plushie by filling the top and bottom rows with black wool, and placing any raw fish between two orange wool in the middle row.

Jellie Cat Plushie

Craft the Jellie cat plushie by filling the top row with gray wool, the bottom row with white wool, and placing any raw fish between two black wool in the middle row.
