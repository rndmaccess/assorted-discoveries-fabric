About

The cow plushie is a decorative plushie inspired by the cow mob!

Config

Set Enable Cow Plushies to Yes/true (default) to activate these blocks, or No/false to disable them. All cow plushies are controlled by this setting.

Crafting Recipe

Craft the temperate cow plushie by filling the top and bottom rows with brown wool, and placing beef between two black wool in the middle row.
