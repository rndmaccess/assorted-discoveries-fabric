About

A dyed campfire is a variant of the campfire that comes in all 16 dye colors.

Config

Set Enable Dyed Campfires to Yes/true (default) to activate these blocks, or No/false to disable them. All dyed campfires are controlled by this setting.

Placing

Dyed campfires are lit by default when placed.

Breaking

When broken, a dyed campfire drops

  • Nothing if mined by hand.
  • 2 Charcoal if mined with any tool.
  • Itself if mined with a tool enchanted with Silk Touch.

Waterlogged

When waterlogged dyed campfires go out, similar to normal campfires, and food cannot be cooked on them. If re-lit with a flint and steel, the dyed campfire will retain its color.

Hoppers

Dyed campfires do not have an external inventory, similar to normal campfires. Raw food cannot be loaded into the dyed campfire with a hopper.

A hopper placed directly underneath a dyed campfire pulls through any items dropped from the dyed campfire. Any drops from a mob that dies in the dyed campfire get pulled into the hopper.

Damage

Dyed campfires will damage players and other mobs, similar to normal campfires. They do not destroy items.

Cooking

Up to 4 food items can be placed on a dyed campfire at once and will cook simultaneously. Any food on a dyed campfire will take 30 seconds (600 ticks) to cook. Once the food is finished cooking it will pop off the campfire.

Particles

Dyed campfires produce smoke particles that float up around 10 blocks before disappearing. If a hay bale is placed below, the dyed campfire becomes a signal fire and the smoke floats up 24 blocks instead. These smoke particles behave just like vanilla campfire smoke.

Dyed Campfires also occasionally emit ember particles, similar to lava. The particle's color matches the dyed campfire's color.

Bees

Placing a campfire within five blocks of a beehive or bee nest allows honey bottles and honeycomb to be harvested without provoking the bees, similar to normal campfires.

Crafting Recipe

Craft a dyed campfire by placing a campfire and any color of dye in any arrangement.
