About

Green onion seeds are items that can be obtained from breaking wild green onions, or more abundantly harvested from green onion crops, and are used to plant them.

Green onion crops are planted in farmland and used to grow green onions and green onion seeds.

Config

Set the Enable Green Onions option to Yes (or true) to use them, or No (or false) to hide them. All green onion blocks, items, and seeds use this option. Yes (or true) is the default setting.

Farming

Fully grown green onion crops drop 1 green onion and 1 to 4 green onion seeds per harvest. Seed yield can be increased using a tool enchanted with Fortune, with Fortune III harvesting on an average of 6.14 green onion seeds per harvest.

Planting

Green onion seeds can be planted on farmland in the tag: minecraft:supports_crops
