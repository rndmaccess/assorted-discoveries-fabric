About

The hoglin plushie and zoglin plushie are decorative plushies inspired by the hoglin mob's various forms!

Config

Set Enable Hoglin Plushie to Yes/true (default) to activate these blocks, or No/false to disable them. Both the hoglin and zoglin plushies are controlled by this setting.

Crafting Recipe

Hoglin Plushie

Craft the hoglin plushie by filling the top and bottom rows with terracotta, and placing a porkchop between two white wool in the middle row.

Zoglin Plushie

Craft the zoglin plushie by filling the top row with white wool, placing a porkchop between two black wool in the middle row, and filling the bottom row with pink wool.

Placement

When placing a hoglin and zoglin plushie, it will face the player.

Breaking

Hoglin and zoglin Plushies can be broken with any item or by hand!
