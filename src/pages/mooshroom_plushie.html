About

The mooshroom plushie is a decorative block inspired by the various mooshroom mob variants!

Config

Set Enable Mooshroom Plushie to Yes/true (default) to activate these blocks, or No/false to disable them. Both the red and brown mooshroom plushies are controlled by this setting.

Placement

When placing any mooshroom plushie, it will face the player.

Breaking

Mooshroom Plushies can be broken with any item or by hand!

Crafting Recipe

Red Mooshroom Plushie

Craft the red mooshroom plushie by filling the top and bottom rows with red wool, and placing a red mushroom between two black wool in the middle row.

Brown Mooshroom Plushie

Craft the brown mooshroom plushie by filling the top and bottom rows with brown wool, and placing a brown mushroom between two black wool in the middle row.
