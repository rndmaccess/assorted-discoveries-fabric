About

The mooshroom plushie is a decorative plushie inspired by the red mooshroom and brown mooshroom mobs!

Config

  • In-Game Screen Name: Enable Mooshroom Plushies
  • Config File Key: enable_mooshroom_plushies
  • Default: true / Yes

Determines whether red and brown mooshroom plushies can be obtained.

  • true / Yes: Red and brown mooshroom plushies are enabled.
  • false / No: Red and brown mooshroom plushies are disabled and hidden.

Crafting Recipe

Red Mooshroom Plushie

Craft the red mooshroom plushie by filling the top and bottom rows with red wool, and placing a red mushroom between two black wool in the middle row.

Brown Mooshroom Plushie

Craft the brown mooshroom plushie by filling the top and bottom rows with brown wool, and placing a brown mushroom between two black wool in the middle row.
