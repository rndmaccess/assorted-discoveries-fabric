About

Cindersnap berries and frostbite berries are food items obtained from cindersnap berry bushes and frostbite berry bushes respectively. You can also use these berries to plant the bushes.

Cindersnap berry bushes and frostbite berry bushes are quick-growing plants that grow cindersnap berries and frostbite berries respectively. These bushes can be harvested to obtain more berries. Players and most mobs are slowed down and take constant damage while moving through these berry bushes. Unlike normal bushes, these cannot be grown or multiplied using bone meal.

Natural Generation

Cindersnap berry bushes and frostbite berry bushes can both be found in the warped forest and crimson forest biomes in the Nether dimension.

Config

Cindersnap bushes and berries

  • In-Game Screen Name: Enable Cindersnap Berries
  • Config File Key: enable_cindersnap_berries
  • Default: true / Yes

Determines whether cindersnap berries and bushes generate naturally in the world and can be obtained.

  • true / Yes: Cindersnap berries and bushes are enabled.
  • false / No: Cindersnap berries and bushes are disabled and hidden.

Frostbite bushes and berries

  • In-Game Screen Name: Enable Frostbite Berries
  • Config File Key: enable_frostbite_berries
  • Default: true / Yes

Determines whether frostbite berries and bushes generate naturally in the world and can be obtained.

  • true / Yes: Frostbite berries and bushes are enabled.
  • false / No: Frostbite berries and bushes are disabled and hidden.

Placing

Cindersnap berries and frostbite berries can be planted on both modded and vanilla netherrack, as well as, warped and crimson nylium. The associated tag for the netherrack blocks is c:netherracks.

Breaking

Both bushes can be mined instantly with any tool or by hand. A mature cindersnap berry bush or frostbite berry bush yields 2–3 berries. On its third growth stage, the bush yields 1–2 berries. Each level of Fortune can increase the amount of drops by 1.

Harvesting

Berries can be collected from a berry bush by pressing the use control on it, yielding 1–2 appropriate berries in its third growth stage, and 2–3 berries in its final growth stage. After dropping the berries on the ground, the berry bush reverts to its second growth stage.

Crafting Recipes

Cindersnap Berry Juice

Craft cindersnap berry juice by placing cindersnap berries in a plus shape around a central glass bottle.

Frostbite Berry Juice

Craft frostbite berry juice by placing frostbite berries in a plus shape around a central glass bottle.

Crimson Forage Mix

Craft crimson forage mix by placing three cindersnap berries and three of any crimson vegetation in any arrangement.

Warped Forage Mix

Craft warped forage mix by placing three frostbite berries and three of any warped vegetation in any arrangement.
