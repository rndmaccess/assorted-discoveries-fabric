About

Cindersnap berry juice is a nether food item that is made from cindersnap berries.

Frostbite berry juice is a nether food item that is made from frostbite berries.

Config

Cindersnap Berry Juice: Set Enable Cindersnap Berry Juice to Yes/true (default) to activate this item, or No/false to disable it. If cindersnap berries are disabled, cindersnap berry juice will be disabled as well. Visit the nether berries page to read more about these berries.

Frostbite Berry Juice: Set Enable Frostbite Berry Juice to Yes/true (default) to activate this item, or No/false to disable it. If frostbite berries are disabled, frostbite berry juice will be disabled as well. Visit the nether berries page to read more about these berries.

Crafting Recipes

Cindersnap Berry Juice

Craft cindersnap berry juice by placing cindersnap berries in a plus shape around a central glass bottle.

Frostbite Berry Juice

Craft Frostbite berry juice by placing frostbite berries in a plus shape around a central glass bottle.
