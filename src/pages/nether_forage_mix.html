About

Crimson forage mix is a nether food item that is made from combining cindersnap berries and crimson forest plants.

Warped forage mix is a nether food item that is made from combining frostbite berries and warped forest plants.

Config

Crimson Forage Mix: Set Enable Crimson Forage Mix to Yes/true (default) to activate this item, or No/false to disable it. If cindersnap berries are disabled, crimson forage mix will be disabled as well. Visit the nether berries page to read more about these berries.

Warped Forage Mix: Set Enable Warped Forage Mix to Yes/true (default) to activate this item, or No/false to disable it. If frostbite berries are disabled, warped forage mix will be disabled as well. Visit the nether berries page to read more about these berries.

Crafting Recipes

Crimson Forage Mix

Craft crimson forage mix by placing three cindersnap berries and three of any crimson vegetation in any arrangement.

Warped Forage Mix

Craft warped forage mix by placing three frostbite berries and three of any warped vegetation in any arrangement.
