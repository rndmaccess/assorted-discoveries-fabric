  • true / Yes: Plushies are enabled.

About

Plushies are decorative blocks that are inspired by Minecraft's various mobs.

Placement

When placing a plushie, it will face the player.

Breaking

Plushies can be broken with any item or by hand!

Allay Plushie

The allay plushie is a plushie inspired by the allay↗ mob. The plushie emits a light level of 10 when placed.

Crafting Recipe

Craft the Allay plushie by filling the top and bottom rows with light blue wool and placing an amethyst shard between two white wool in the middle row.

Cow Plushie

The cow plushie is a decorative plushie inspired by the cow↗ mob.

Crafting Recipe

Craft the temperate cow plushie by filling the top and bottom rows with brown wool, and placing beef between two black wool in the middle row.

Mooshroom Plushies

The mooshroom plushie is a decorative plushie inspired by the red mooshroom↗ and brown mooshroom↗ mobs!

Crafting Recipe

Craft the red mooshroom plushie by filling the top and bottom rows with red wool, and placing a red mushroom between two black wool in the middle row.

Craft the brown mooshroom plushie by filling the top and bottom rows with brown wool, and placing a brown mushroom between two black wool in the middle row.

Strider Plushies

The strider plushie and shivering strider plushie are decorative plushies inspired by the strider↗ mob's various forms!

Crafting Recipe

Craft the strider plushie by filling the top and bottom rows with red wool, and placing a lava bucket between two black wool in the middle row.

Craft the shivering strider plushie by filling the top and bottom rows with purple wool, and placing a water bucket between two black wool in the middle row.

Enderman Plushie

The enderman plushie is a decorative plushie inspired by the enderman↗ mob. The plushie’s grass block changes color to match its current biome.

Crafting Recipe

Craft the enderman plushie by filling the top row with black wool, placing an ender pearl between two magenta wool in the middle row, and placing a grass block between two black wool in the bottom row.

Hoglin Plushies

The hoglin plushie and zoglin plushie are decorative plushies inspired by the hoglin↗ and zoglin↗ mob.

Crafting Recipe

Craft the hoglin plushie by filling the top and bottom rows with terracotta, and placing a porkchop between two white wool in the middle row.

Craft the zoglin plushie by filling the top row with white wool, placing a porkchop between two black wool in the middle row, and filling the bottom row with pink wool.
