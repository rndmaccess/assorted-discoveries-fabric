About

The rope ladder is a decorative block that can be hung from blocks!

Config

Set Enable Rope Ladders to Yes/true (default) to activate these blocks, or No/false to disable them. All rope ladders are controlled by this setting.

Extension

To extend any rope ladder simply place the rope ladder while targeting another rope ladder of the same type. There is a video demo showing this at the bottom of this page! Rope ladders can only be extended up to 16 blocks away from a support block. Once this limit is reached their must be another supporting block placed behind the ladder.

Breaking

Rope ladders can be broken with any item or by hand, but the axe is the fastest! Any rope ladders that are unsupported will break when the supporting block is removed.

Crafting Recipe

3

Craft three rope ladders by placing string in the corners and any planks in the middle row.
