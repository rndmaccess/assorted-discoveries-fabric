About

Spruce cones are an early game food item that can be used to craft Forest's Bounty.

Obtaining

Spruce cones can be obtained by breaking spruce leaves. The leaves will drop between 1 and 2 spruce cones; the amount is not increased by Fortune. However, the drop chance is affected. If the leaves are exploded by a creeper, the chances dwindle greatly. This is summarized in the following table:

Fortune Level Drop Chance
0 (No fortune) 2%
1 2.3%
2 2.5%
3 3.5%

Config

Spruce cones are enabled/disabled indirectly with the Enable Forests Bounty config setting. Visit the forest's bounty page for more information.

Crafting Recipes

Craft forest's bounty by placing a bowl, sweet berries, and a spruce cone in any arrangement.
