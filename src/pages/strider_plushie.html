About

The strider and shivering strider plushies are decorative blocks inspired by the strider mob's various forms!

Config

Set Enable Strider Plushies to Yes/true (default) to activate these blocks, or No/false to disable them. All strider plushies are controlled by this setting.

Crafting Recipe

Strider Plushie

Craft the strider plushie by filling the top and bottom rows with red wool, and placing a lava bucket between two black wool in the middle row.

Shivering Strider Plushie

Craft the shivering strider plushie by filling the top and bottom rows with purple wool, and placing a water bucket between two black wool in the middle row.

Placement

When placing a strider plushie, it will face the player.

Breaking

Strider Plushies can be broken with any item or by hand!
