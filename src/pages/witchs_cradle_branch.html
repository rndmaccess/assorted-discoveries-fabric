About

Witch's cradle branches are food items obtained from witch's cradles. You can also use these branches to plant more cradles.

Witch's cradles are quick-growing plants that grow witch's cradle branches. These cradles can be harvested to obtain more branches. Players and most mobs are slowed down and take constant damage while moving through witch's cradles.

Config

  • In-Game Screen Name: Enable Witch's Cradles
  • Config File Key: enable_witchs_cradles
  • Default: true / Yes

Determines whether witch's cradles generate naturally in the world, and if witch's cradle branches, witch's cradle soup, and witch's cradles can be obtained.

  • true / Yes: Witch's cradles, witch's cradle branches and witch's cradle soup are enabled.
  • false / No: Witch's cradles, witch's cradle branches and witch's cradle soup are disabled and hidden.

Natural Generation

Witch's cradles can be found in both modded and unmodded swamps. Without other mods installed, this is limited to the swamp and mangrove swamp.

Placing

Witch's cradles can be planted on both modded and vanilla soil blocks. The tag that this plant uses is minecraft:supports_vegetation. The full list without other mods installed is as follows:

  • dirt
  • grass blocks
  • moss blocks
  • mud
  • farmland
  • planter boxes
  • dirt slabs
  • grass slabs

Breaking

Witch's cradles can be mined instantly with any tool or by hand. A mature witch's cradle yields 2–3 branches. On its third growth stage, the bush yields 1–2 branches. Each level of Fortune can increase the amount of drops by 1.

Harvesting

Witch's cradle branches can be collected from a witch's cradle by pressing the use control on it, yielding 1–2 branches in its third growth stage, and 2–3 branches in its final growth stage. After dropping the branches on the ground, the cradle reverts to its second growth stage.

Crafting Recipes

Craft witch's cradle soup by placing six witch's cradle branches and a bowl in any arrangement.
