About

The wolf plushies are decorative plushies inspired by the various wolf mob variants!

Config

Set Enable Wolf Plushies to Yes/true (default) to activate these blocks, or No/false to disable them. All wolf plushies are controlled by this setting.

Placement

When placing any wolf plushie, it will face the player.

Breaking

Wolf Plushies can be broken with any item or by hand!

Interaction

A wolf plushie can alternate between sitting and standing by using the "Use Item/Place Block" control (default: right mouse button). To place a block against the plushie instead, you must sneak.

Crafting Recipe

Pale Wolf Plushie

Craft the pale wolf plushie by filling the top and bottom rows with white wool, and placing a bone between two black wool in the middle row.

Ashen Wolf Plushie

Craft the ashen wolf plushie by placing three light gray wool in the top row; a bone between two black wool in the middle row; and three white wool in the bottom row.

Black Wolf Plushie

Craft the black wolf plushie by filling the top and bottom rows with black wool, and placing a bone between two gray wool in the middle row.

Chestnut Wolf Plushie

Craft the chestnut wolf plushie by filling the top and bottom rows with brown wool, and placing a bone between two black wool in the middle row.

Rusty Wolf Plushie

Craft the rusty wolf plushie by placing three brown wool in the top row; a bone between two black wool in the middle row; and three white wool in the bottom row.
